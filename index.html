Questions we get often

How do I get in touch?

Message us on WhatsApp or send an email. Tell us what you need and we will agree a time that suits you.

How much will it cost?

The price depends on the garment, the style and the fabric. We can give you a quote once we know what you want.

How long does it take?

Timing depends on the garment and how busy we are. Message us with your date and we will tell you what is realistic.

Can you alter clothes I bought elsewhere?

Yes. We do alterations and repairs to make clothes fit well and last longer.

Do you make uniforms for businesses?

Yes. We make custom corporate attire and uniforms to match your business image.
